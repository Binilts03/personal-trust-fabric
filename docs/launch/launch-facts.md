**Do not claim DPDP requires a record of processing activities.** The strings
"record of processing" and "records of processing" do not appear in the Act.
That obligation is a GDPR Article 30 idea.

## x402 (the HTTP 402 agent-payment protocol)

| Fact                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | Source                                                                                                                                                                        |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| The protocol is now under the x402 Foundation, launched operationally under the Linux Foundation on 14 July 2026 with 40 member organisations; Premier members include Adyen, AWS, American Express, Circle, Cloudflare, Coinbase, Fiserv, Google, Mastercard, MoonPay, Ripple, Shopify, Solana Foundation, Stellar, Stripe and Visa. | https://www.linuxfoundation.org/press/linux-foundation-announces-operational-launch-of-x402-foundation-to-standardize-internet-native-payments-for-ai-agents-and-applications |
| The current specification is Protocol Version 2, dated 9 December 2025.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | https://github.com/x402-foundation/x402 (`specs/x402-specification-v2.md`)                                                                                                    |
| The HTTP transport uses three headers: `PAYMENT-REQUIRED` (server to client, base64 `PaymentRequired`), `PAYMENT-SIGNATURE` (client to server, base64 `PaymentPayload`), `PAYMENT-RESPONSE` (server to client, base64 `SettlementResponse`).                                                                                          | `specs/transports-v2/http.md`                                                                                                                                                 |
| The v1 header names (`X-PAYMENT`, `X-PAYMENT-RESPONSE`) are superseded. `WWW-Authenticate` appears nowhere in any version of the spec or the docs.                                                                                                                                                                                    | `specs/transports-v1/http.md`, the v1 to v2 migration guide, and a full-text search of the docs corpus (0 hits)                                                               |
| The amount is a decimal string in the asset's atomic units; the core spec does not define the decimals for an asset.                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | `specs/x402-specification-v2.md` s.5.1                                                                                                                                        |
| The facilitator verifies and settles; it does not hold funds. The resource server re-checks the payload against its own requirements.                                                                                                                                                                                                 | `specs/x402-specification-v2.md` s.7                                                                                                                                          |

## Unverified, do not use in a post

- The x402 usage counters (75.41M transactions, $24.24M volume, 94.06K
  buyers, 22K sellers): self-reported by the project, no published date, no
  methodology.
- The Visa Trust Index for agentic commerce (23% of US consumers trust GenAI
  to handle payments; 61% trust Visa specifically): a Visa-commissioned
  Harris Poll survey, fielded 26-28 May 2026, n=2,065. Label it as a
  commissioned survey if it is used at all.
- "4,700% surge in AI-driven traffic to US retail sites" (Visa's own press
  release headline, 14 October 2025): a vendor claim with no verified
  measurement.
- Stripe's projected non-human transaction share (22% by end of 2026, 40% by
  2028): attributed to a Stripe annual report by a third-party aggregator. The
  report was not obtained.
- "OLAS logs 14M agent deals" and "Mastercard Agent Pay tops 30 firms": a
  Forbes contributor blog, not verified against OLAS or Mastercard directly.
- The Washington Post piece of 2 October 2026 on rogue agents: only the
  search-result snippet was seen. Verify before using it.
- Whether the Data Protection Board has appointed a Chairperson or Members:
  the primary sources do not report an appointment, and a secondary source
  claiming a 6 June 2026 appointment is contradicted by the primary sources.
- Any number of personal-data-breach intimations or complaints received since
  November 2025: the Government's answer to the question did not give a
  number.
- Whether any Consent Manager has been registered: no register was published
  and the Government's answer did not state one.
- The Data Protection Board's member count: G.S.R. 845(E) says four members;
  the parliamentary answer and the recruitment advertisement say a
  Chairperson and four members.
