# Code of Conduct

PTF aims to become shared infrastructure for agentic commerce. That only
works if reviewers, contributors, and operators — human and agent — can
trust the room as well as the code.

## Standard

Be precise over performative: cite files, lines, and tests instead of
trading adjectives. Disagree with ideas freely; do not attack people.
Assume a confusing review process is a docs bug, and file it.

Unacceptable: harassment, intimidation, or threats of any kind — including
"or else" pressure around merges, releases, or reviews. Urgency never
justifies abuse.

## Scope

Applies in issues, pull requests, reviews, and any project space,
regardless of whether you participate as a person or through an agent.

## Enforcement

Violations may lead to warnings, temporary interaction limits, or bans,
decided by the maintainer and recorded privately. Report privately per
`SECURITY.md` (security contact doubles as conduct contact until a team
exists); public call-outs are not required and often escalate.

## Attribution

Adapted from the spirit of the Contributor Covenant: our standards are
higher for maintainers, who set the tone in every review.
